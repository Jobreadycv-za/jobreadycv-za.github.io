FAQ

Questions people ask before they start.

Ask on WhatsApp
Is this actually built for South African CVs?

Yes. We default to SA English, 2–3 page professional CVs, a references block, and the sections local hiring managers expect. We also understand graduate programmes, learnerships, and how equity language shows up in SA adverts — without turning your document into a form.

Will my CV pass applicant tracking systems?

Documents are structured with a single-column reading order, standard headings, and no text locked inside images. We write against the parsers used by large SA employers — SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle, Workday, and common PNet / Careers24 ingest paths.

Can I use my existing CV?

Yes. Send a PDF or Word file on WhatsApp. We'll extract the story, clean the structure, and rewrite it so it reads as one document — not a collage of old jobs.

Do the cover letters sound like AI?

Only if you accept the first draft unedited — which we don't recommend. The letter stays specific: company name, role, and your actual evidence. You adjust tone, cut lines, and keep the sentences that sound like you.

How do I start?

Tap any WhatsApp button on this page. Send the pre-filled note (or write your own), share your current CV or a job ad if you have one, and we'll get a first draft moving. No account, no card on the site — we confirm the kit and payment on the chat.

How does pricing work in rand?

Each kit is a once-off payment in ZAR: Student R150, Standard R450, Premium R950. The USD figures on the page are approximate conversions, not a fixed second price. We confirm the tier and payment details on WhatsApp before any work starts. Nothing renews.

What happens to my personal information?

We only keep what you send us — your name, contact details and CV content — and we use it solely to build your documents. We don't sell CVs or data to job boards, and you can ask us to delete everything at any time. See the privacy notice for the full picture under POPIA.
