Who we are

jobready.za is a CV-writing and career-services provider operating in South Africa, currently reachable at jobreadycv-za.github.io and via WhatsApp at +27 68 251 0828. We are the "responsible party" for personal information you share with us.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us — usually over WhatsApp:

  • Your name and contact details (WhatsApp number, email if provided);
  • The content of your CV, cover letters and any job adverts you share;
  • Basic context about your job search (role, city, industry).

We do not collect your information from third parties, and we don't use tracking pixels or advertising cookies on this site.

Why we process it

  • To write, edit and tailor your CV, cover letter and LinkedIn profile (the service you asked for);
  • To communicate with you about your documents; and
  • To provide the ATS review you request.

Sharing

We never sell personal information and we never share CVs with job boards or employers without your explicit instruction. We only disclose information where legally required.

Retention

We keep your documents and messages for as long as we're actively working with you, and delete or anonymise them when you ask or after you've ended the relationship.

Your rights

  • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of your data at any time; and
  • Object to or restrict processing.

To exercise any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp at +27 68 251 0828 and ask for the data request. We respond within a reasonable time and always confirm deletion.

Contact
